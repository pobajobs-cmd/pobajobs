How to measure
  1. Stand back far enough to see the top of the pot and the bottom of the wall below it. Further back is more accurate.
  2. Shoot the pot, then the base of the wall straight below it, without moving.
  3. Same level as the house? That's it: use Flat ground.
  4. Up or down steps? Add a third shot held dead level at the wall (Steps or slope). Or note both angles (Angles), from the laser or your phone.
  5. Phone tilt: lay the phone flat on top of the laser, screen up, top of the phone pointing where the laser points. Aim, fire the laser, hold still, tap Lock.
