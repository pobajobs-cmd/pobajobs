How to measure
  1. Stand back far enough to see the top of the pot and the bottom of the wall below it. Further back is more accurate.
  2. Shoot the pot. Hit the top of the pot, or the stack just under it.
  3. Shoot the base of the wall straight below the chimney, without moving. Or use Level to wall and shoot straight ahead, level.
  4. If your laser shows a tilt angle, Angle needs just the pot shot.
