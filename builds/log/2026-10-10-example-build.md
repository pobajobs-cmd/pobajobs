## How it went

The calibration cube is the "hello world" of 3D printing: a 20 mm cube with X, Y and Z marked on the sides. Print it, measure it, and you'll know if your printer is telling the truth.

1. Slice it at 0.2 mm layers with 15% infill.
2. Print it. Make a cup of tea.
3. Measure each side. Anything within a couple of tenths of a millimetre is fine.

Tolerance: &plusmn; a bit.
